Frequently Asked Questions

You can book a vehicle through our online form, by calling our phone number, or by visiting our location.
To rent a car, you need a valid passport, a driver’s license, and an entry stamp for Bosnia and Herzegovina. If you are a citizen of BiH, a national ID card and a driver’s license are required.
The minimum age to rent a vehicle is 21, with a valid driver’s license held for at least 2 years.
There is no mileage limit. However, it is necessary to notify us in advance if you plan to drive a larger number of kilometers.
Vehicles are insured, and the level of insurance depends on the price.
The vehicle can be picked up at one location and, with prior notice and an additional fee, returned at another location. The amount of the fee depends on the return location.
In case of a traffic accident, the insurance terms are outlined in the contract you receive before renting the vehicle. If the contract terms are not respected, the driver bears the costs. Costs of consumables and breakdowns not caused by the driver's fault are covered by the rental company.
Vehicles are rented with the existing fuel level and must be returned in the same condition.
